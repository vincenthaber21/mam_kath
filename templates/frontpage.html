Workflow

Three steps from photo to verified record

Every stage stays visible so researchers can inspect, correct and keep a clear audit trail.

  1. 01

    Upload an incident photo

    Choose a JPG or PNG, or capture one with a phone camera. Add an optional reference label when testing.

    One image · maximum 10 MB
  2. 02

    Review the model output

    The active model returns one predicted category, a confidence score, all five class scores and inference time.

    Scores below 70% are left unassigned
  3. 03

    Verify and keep the record

    A reviewer confirms or corrects the prediction. The result is stored under Incidents for filtering, export and evaluation.

    Human judgment stays in control

Taxonomy

Five incident types. One label each.

The model assigns exactly one category. It does not assess severity or dispatch assistance.

  • 01Fire

    Flames, smoke and fire-related scenes.

  • 02Crime

    Criminal incidents or police response.

  • 03Medical emergency

    Urgent care and medical assistance.

  • 04Accident

    Road, vehicle and accidental scenes.

  • 05Disaster

    Floods, storms and disaster damage.

Responsible research

AI assistance, not emergency decision-making.

Predictions are supplementary information and always require human verification. This prototype does not contact emergency services, notify agencies, detect locations, or assess incident severity.
