A clear, reviewable workflow

How the classifier works

From upload to verified record, every stage remains visible to the researcher.

01

Upload an incident photo

Choose a JPG or PNG image, or capture one with a phone camera. Add an optional reference label when testing.

One image · maximum 10 MB
02

Review the model output

The active model returns one predicted category, a confidence score, all five class scores and inference time.

Scores below 60% are flagged
03

Verify and keep the record

A reviewer confirms or corrects the prediction. The result is stored in History for filtering, export and evaluation.

Human judgment stays in control

Five focused classes

One system. Five incident types.

Each image receives exactly one label. The model does not assign severity or dispatch assistance.

01

Fire

Visible flames, smoke and fire-related incident scenes.

02

Crime

Images associated with criminal incidents or police response.

03

Medical emergency

Medical incidents requiring urgent care or assistance.

04

Accident

Road, vehicle and other accidental incident scenes.

05

Disaster

Floods, storms, earthquakes and other disaster damage.

Designed for responsible research

AI assistance, not emergency decision-making.

Predictions are supplementary information and always require human verification. This prototype does not contact emergency services, notify agencies, detect locations, or assess incident severity.
